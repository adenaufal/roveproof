# Naming decision: Roveproof

**Working product name:** **Roveproof**  
**Category descriptor:** Real-world journey CI  
**Tagline:** **Prove every journey in the real world.**

## Why it wins

`Rove` evokes journeys across varied conditions; `proof` captures evidence, independent reruns, and human-gated confidence. The name is global, pronounceable, developer-credible, and not limited to Indonesia or checkout.

Launch treatment:

> **Roveproof**  
> Real-world journey CI  
> Prove every journey in the real world.

Positioning bridge:

> Built in Indonesia, Roveproof tests the conditions software meets everywhere.

## Preliminary validation

A constrained exact-name search found no evident software-company match and no indexed result for the obvious `.com`, `.dev`, or `.ai` strings. DNS non-resolution was observed for `roveproof.com` at check time. These are encouraging signals, **not** legal or registrar clearance.

Before public commitment, perform an authoritative registrar/RDAP check, package/social-handle sweep, and professional trademark similarity search. Backups: `Veridrome`, then `Varyscape`.
